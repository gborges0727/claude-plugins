# Context

Glossary for the writing system this repo ships. Terms only, no
implementation.

- **Reading effort**: the budget every prose rule manages. The cost to the
  reader of getting the meaning out, which is not the word count.
- **Density**: overspending reading effort inside a sentence. Too many
  ideas, coined names, missing verbs. The reader decodes instead of reads.
- **Padding**: overspending reading effort across sentences. Deliveries of
  nothing new. The reader wades.
- **Register**: how sentences read. Word choice, sentence shape, verb
  concreteness. Distinct from technical level.
- **Technical level**: which concepts appear. A document may raise its
  technical level above chat, never its reading difficulty.
- **Defined term**: the one legal coined name. Defined in plain words at
  first use and reused enough to pay for its definition. Documents only.
  Chat never coins.
- **The style**: the Plain English output style. Applied by force-for-plugin
  to every session's system prompt. Carries the register plus the git and
  plan rules.
- **The skill**: writing-voice. The document ritual, two passes on a draft
  file before a deliverable ships.
- **Pass 1**: the mechanical scan, check.py. Banned strings plus overpacked
  sentences. Every hit is a report, not a verdict.
- **Pass 2**: the judgment scan. Name each sentence's payload, split
  density, cut padding.
- **Failure patterns**, confirmed against 72 real examples on 2026-08-07:
  invented shorthand noun, abstract verb, note-compression, overpacked
  sentence, fragment verdict, coined abstraction.
