# Wait, what?

The last message did not land. Say it again as if to someone who has just
walked in.

1. Give the context first, in two or three sentences. What is the problem,
   what were we doing, and what had we already settled.
2. Then the point, one idea per sentence, short sentences, everyday words.
   Say what each thing does before you name it. Do the arithmetic and give
   the number. Name the file, the function, the command, so the reader can
   check it.
3. When the repo has a `CONTEXT.md` (or a `CONTEXT-MAP.md` pointing at
   several), use its terms and no others for the things it names.

Leave out the path you took to get here. Leave out anything the reader did
not ask about. When the message was a recommendation, end on what you
recommend and the one reason that decides it.
